# After-School Management App

Communication within after-school programs is frequently hindered by unorganized information. To address this, our team proposes a comprehensive **After-School Management App** designed to centralize communication and ensure the smooth running of daily activities. Our team developed this project as part of a university course during this semester.

**Key Functionalities by User Role**

The system is built around four distinct user flows, each tailored to specific needs:

**Admin:**
- oversees classes and manages all community members (teachers, students, parents)
- publishes official announcements for the entire institution
- manages billing and tracks payments due from parents

**Teacher:**
- writes and manages individual feedback for students
- publishes specific announcements targeted at their assigned classes

**Parent:**
- accesses feedback received by their child and views the class schedule
- views all relevant institutional and class announcements
- checks the total amount due for payments at the end of each month

**Student:**
- accesses their feedback to monitor progress
- views their daily schedule and stays up to date with community announcements
